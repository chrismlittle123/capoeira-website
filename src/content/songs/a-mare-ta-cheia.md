## Coro
A maré tá cheia, ioiô
> The tide is full, ioiô
A maré tá cheia, iaiá (x2)
> The tide is full, iaiá

## Solo
A maré subiu
> The tide came in
+ Sobe, maré
> Rise, tide
A maré desceu
> The tide went out
+ Desce, maré
> Fall, tide
Ô, maré é de maré
> Oh, the tide is the tide
Vou pra Ilha de Maré
> I'm going to Ilha de Maré
