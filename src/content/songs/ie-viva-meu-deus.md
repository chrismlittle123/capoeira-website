about: |
  Each line is sung by the soloist and repeated by the coro with "camará"
  added. The verses vary from roda to roda — the soloist can praise whatever
  they like. In many rodas a verse such as "Iê, vamos embora" or "Iê, é hora,
  é hora" signals that the louvação is over and the game can begin.

## Solo & coro
Iê, viva meu Deus
> Hail, long live my God
+ Iê, viva meu Deus, camará
> Hail, long live my God, comrade

Iê, viva meu mestre
> Hail, long live my master
+ Iê, viva meu mestre, camará
> Hail, long live my master, comrade

Iê, quem me ensinou
> Hail to the one who taught me
+ Iê, quem me ensinou, camará
> Hail to the one who taught me, comrade

Iê, a capoeira
> Hail to capoeira
+ Iê, a capoeira, camará
> Hail to capoeira, comrade

Iê, volta do mundo
> Hail, the world turns
+ Iê, volta do mundo, camará
> Hail, the world turns, comrade
