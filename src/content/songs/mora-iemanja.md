## Solo
Quando a maré baixar
> When the tide goes out
Vá lhe visitar
> Go and visit her
Vá lhe fazer devoção
> Go and pay her devotion
Vá lhe presentear
> Go and bring her gifts
+ No mar, mora Iemanjá (x3)
> In the sea lives Iemanjá

Vários negros foram pro Brasil
> Many Black people were taken to Brazil
Bantos, nagôs e iorubás
> Bantu, Nagô and Yoruba
Dentro do navio negreiro
> Inside the slave ship
Deixaram suas lágrimas correr no mar
> They let their tears run into the sea
+ No mar, mora Iemanjá (x3)
> In the sea lives Iemanjá

Sua lágrima que correu no mar
> Their tears that ran into the sea
Tocou no peito de Iemanjá
> Touched the heart of Iemanjá
Ela podia mudar a maré
> She could change the tide
Fazer meu navio voltar pra Guiné
> And make my ship sail back to Guinea
+ No mar, mora Iemanjá (x3)
> In the sea lives Iemanjá
