  sailor" answers that it was the tumbling of the ship or the swaying of the sea
  that taught him to swim — learned by living it, like capoeira itself. The
  verses can be repeated in any order; end on "Marinheiro só".

## Solo & coro
Eu não sou daqui
> I'm not from here
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
Eu não tenho amor
> I don't have a love
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
Eu sou da Bahia
> I'm from Bahia
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
E de São Salvador
> And from São Salvador
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor

Ô marinheiro, marinheiro
> Oh sailor, sailor
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
Quem te ensinou a nadar? (or: a navegar?)
> Who taught you to swim? (or: to sail?)
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
Foi o tombo do navio
> Was it the tumbling of the ship
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
Ou foi o balanço do mar?
> Or the swaying of the sea?
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor

Lá vem, lá vem
> Here he comes, here he comes
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
Como ele vem faceiro
> How cheerful he looks as he comes
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
Todo de branco
> All dressed in white
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
Com seu bonezinho
> With his little cap
+ Marinheiro só
> Lonely sailor
