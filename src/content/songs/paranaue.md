  One of the best-known capoeira songs anywhere in the world. "Paraná" is often
  said to refer to the Paraguayan War (1864–1870), when many capoeiristas were
  sent to fight — the singer tells his wife that capoeira has "beaten him",
  i.e. called him away. Expect many local variations of the solo verses.
