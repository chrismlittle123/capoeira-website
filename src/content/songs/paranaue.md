about: |
  One of the best-known capoeira songs anywhere in the world. "Paraná" is often
  said to refer to the Paraguayan War (1864–1870), when many capoeiristas were
  sent to fight. "Capoeira me venceu" is usually read as "capoeira has won me
  over" or "got the better of me". Expect many local variations of the solo
  verses.
