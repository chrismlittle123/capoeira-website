summary: Play beautifully, I want to see — and learn.
about: |
  A favourite for encouraging a beautiful game. Aidê is often explained as an
  enslaved woman who refused her master and ran away to the quilombo; in the
  roda the coro simply cheers the players on. The solo verses are free: sing
  these, make up your own, or just call "ai, ai, Aidê" in its variations.

## Coro
Ai, ai, Aidê
> Oh, oh, Aidê
Joga bonito que eu quero ver
> Play beautifully, I want to see
Ai, ai, Aidê
> Oh, oh, Aidê
Joga bonito que eu quero aprender
> Play beautifully, I want to learn
